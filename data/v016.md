## Smart Enter — try it in edit mode

Place the cursor at the end of any list line below and press Enter:

- first bullet
- second bullet
- third bullet

Or an ordered list (Enter increments the number):

1. one
2. two
3. three

Quote continuation:

> a quote line
> another quote line

Empty bullet outdents:

- type Enter on an empty bullet
-

The trailing empty `- ` above will outdent on Enter, leaving a plain
new paragraph.
